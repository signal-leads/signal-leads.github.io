Why this is worth trusting.

      
I am Shaun Finch. I am based in Manchester and I have spent ten years working in the tech space. Signal is my own business — not a call centre, not a reseller, not a franchise. You deal with me directly, and if something is wrong you tell me and I sort it that day.


      

        
Where the leads come from

        
Public posts on social media — the local groups, community pages and noticeboards covering your area. The kind of thing anybody nearby can read. Nothing private, nothing bought in from a third party, nothing out of anyone's inbox. We are not finding these anywhere you could not look yourself, if you had six hours a day to sit there looking.

      


      

        
One client per trade, per area

        
If you take plumbing in your town, nobody else gets plumbing in your town. The same post does not go out to five of you to race each other to the phone, because that is the trick that makes most lead services worthless. It also means a patch can only be sold once, which is the honest reason the price is what it is.

      


      

        
Nobody can honestly promise you volume

        
Some weeks a lot of people post about the thing you do. Some weeks hardly anyone does. It moves with the weather, the time of year, the size of your patch and who happens to be typing. The tiers below cap how much gets sent to you — they are not a promise that the work will be there. Anyone quoting you a guaranteed number before they have watched your area for a week has made it up.

      


      

        
Nothing to install

        
No software on your computer, no app to learn, no dashboard, no password to forget. If you have Telegram on your phone, you are set up. If you have not, it takes two minutes and it is free.
